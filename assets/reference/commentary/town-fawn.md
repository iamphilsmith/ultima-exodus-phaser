Fawn is the Snake shrine town — signs reference <Pray> for the Silver Snake invocation and the Circle of Light. A few things stand out in the NPC data:

Two rows of four sequential tile types (NPCs 24–27 at y=18, NPCs 28–31 at y=44) — tiles 20, 21, 22, 23 in order. These are almost certainly the four cardinal direction statues or shrine guardians arranged symmetrically, a hallmark of Fawn's layout
Four tile-23 NPCs in a row at y=48 (NPCs 20–23) — likely an altar row flanking a merchant
NPC 32 is the sole dialog-6 NPC, a wandering tile-23 at (16, 32) — probably the town's key quest-giver
3 merchants (NPCs 6, 10, 11) and no hostile NPCs — Fawn is one of the friendlier towns
7 wandering tile-18 NPCs (12–17) spread around the map give it a lively feel
