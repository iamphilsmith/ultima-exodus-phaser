Montor East is the Cards town — signs reference inserting cards into panels, a core endgame mechanic. Notable layout details:

20 wandering tile-18 NPCs (1–20) with no dialog — by far the most anonymous population of any town so far, arranged in four clusters of four at the map's quadrant corners plus two pairs along the left edge. Very symmetrical, gives the town a bustling market feel
NPCs 21–24 are four stationary tile-16 guards in two pairs flanking the top entrance (y=9–10)
NPC 28 (tile-17, stationary, dialog 3) at dead centre (32, 32) — the central authority figure of the town
NPCs 29–32 are four stationary tile-20 NPCs at the four quadrant midpoints — likely the panel operators for the card insertion mechanic
Only 1 merchant (NPC 25) and no hostile NPCs — a peaceful, puzzle-focused town
