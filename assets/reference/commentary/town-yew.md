Yew is notably sparse compared to the others — only 21 NPCs (all tile-21, bar 2 merchants), only 4 signs, and the fewest unique tile types of any town. It has a monastic, contemplative feel:

Sign 1 — "Welcome to the Circle of Light!" — Yew is the shrine town for the Mark of Force invocation
Sign 2 — "Bonim Anima Teuri, Amen!" — pseudo-Latin liturgical flavour text, unique in the game
13 wandering tile-21 NPCs sharing dialog 3 (NPCs 6–13, 16–17) — a congregation rather than a population
4 stationary tile-21 NPCs at the four corners of a rectangle (28–44, 22–39) — almost certainly the cardinal compass points of the Circle of Light
NPC 1 is the sole dialog-1 NPC, wandering alone at (22, 32) — the town elder or high priest
Only 2 merchants, no hostile NPCs
