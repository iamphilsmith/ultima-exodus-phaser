Castle Death lives up to its name. The NPC data is striking:

All but 4 NPCs are hostile — NPCs 9–32 are all flagged hostile, covering a wide variety of monster tile types (8, 9, 13, 14, 18, 28, 29, 30). This is the most combat-heavy location in the game
Only 5 dialog NPCs: two wandering tile-27 guards (dialog 1), a tile-30 wanderer (dialog 2), a single merchant at (32, 25) — deep inside, presumably the imprisoned prisoner mechanic — and two stationary tile-23 figures (dialog 5)
Tile 63 appears here for the first time (NPCs 5–6, wandering at the flanks) — almost certainly Exodus itself or its avatar
NPCs 31–32 are hostile tile-1 (grass tile) at the bottom corners — these are likely the "floor under NPC" being misread, or placeholder sentinels at the exit
Signs set the tone perfectly: "All may enter, None shall leave", "Death is here!", "Leave quickly!"
